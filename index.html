An intense story

Artificial Era - The Dark Time of Machines leaves the reader anxious with certain situations that take place in a catastrophic scenario consumed by destruction. And this leads us to reflect: will we one day witness the much-feared technological singularity?

Information about the author

Diego Souza is passionate about technology and works with Web Development. He is also a lifelong fan of science fiction and a writer in his spare time. A true native of Rio, he loves Rio de Janeiro, the city where he was born.

The book The Artificial Era presents an apocalyptic scenario in which robots achieved their independence and thus became the dominant species on the planet. How can humanity face this powerful enemy?

These and many more details can be found in this science fiction story that promises to make you reflect on the future of humanity!
